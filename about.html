Who We Are

Bringing Freshness, Beauty and Quality to Every Bloom

Option One Agencies is a Kenyan floral business established in 2010 and based in Nairobi. We have built our reputation on quality products, reliable customer service, and a commitment to delivering flowers that remain fresh and beautiful for longer. Our growth has come largely through referrals, with professionalism, consistency, and customer satisfaction at the heart of what we do.

Based in Nairobi, our experience in the floral market helps us understand unique customer needs and provide products and arrangements tailored to each preference.

Our Mission

To present the local market with great and innovative products, delivering beyond expectations, all the time.

Our Vision

To be the supplier of choice for floral products.

What We Do

Fresh-Cut Flowers, Arrangements & Foliage

We offer a wide selection of fresh-cut flowers and foliage — including roses, lilies, gerberas, chrysanthemums, carnations, orchids, anthuriums, heliconias, delphiniums, eucalyptus and many more. We also provide custom floral arrangements designed according to our clients' tastes, occasions, and chosen designs.

Trusted By

Trusted by Our Esteemed Clients

We are proud to have served established hospitality and wholesale customers across Kenya and beyond.

Notable clients include Sarova Pan-Afric Hotel, Sarova Stanley Hotel, Sarova Lion Hill Game Lodge, Serena Hotels, and wholesale customers in the Dar es Salaam market. We look forward to expanding our reach and establishing a stronger regional presence — while continuing to provide the quality floral products and service our clients have come to expect.
